Cultural Decorative Item

Designing and fabricating a multi-part engineered decorative artifact celebrating cultural roots.

← All projects

Overview

Tasked with developing a multi-part decorative item that represented my cultural roots. The project emphasized aesthetic integration, design for manufacturing (DFM), and precision multi-component assembly fit tolerances.

My Contribution

  • Conceived and modeled all individual structural and aesthetic components from scratch using SolidWorks.
  • Designed mechanical joints and interlocks to connect all components together securely without permanent chemical adhesives.
  • Executed 3D printing parameters, managed slicing settings, and successfully finished, assembled, and tested all physical components.
