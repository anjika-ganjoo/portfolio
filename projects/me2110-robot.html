ME 2110 Competition Robot

Designing, prototyping, building, and programming a fully autonomous mechatronic robot for a 40-second competition match.

← All projects

Overview

In ME 2110 (Creative Decisions and Design), our engineering team was tasked with designing, prototyping, manufacturing, and programming a fully autonomous competition robot from scratch. The system had to reliably execute a sequence of complex scoring tasks on a playing field within a strict 40-second competition match window, operating under weight, material, volume, and energy budget constraints.

My Contribution

  • Drivetrain Design & Kinematics: Engineered the complete drivetrain and chassis, ensuring structural stability for other subsystems, precise alignment, and efficient speed transitions during autonomous runs.
  • Subsystem Iteration & Mechanisms: Assisted in brainstorming, prototyping, and refining mechanism sub-assemblies across all point scoring tasks to maximize points scored.
  • CAD Modeling & Assembly Fit: Created full assembly models in SolidWorks to verify component clearances, assembly functionality, and subsystem packaging within the starting box constraints.
  • Prototyping & Fabrication: Managed hands-on manufacturing using laser cutting, 3D printing, machining, and component wiring.
  • Autonomous Integration: Tested motor actuation, pneumatic solenoid timing, and mechanical sensor feedback loops to ensure consistent performance during runs.
