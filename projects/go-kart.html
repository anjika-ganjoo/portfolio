ME 1670 Educational Go Kart

Conceptualizing an interactive STEM kit designed to teach young children mechanical assembly, energy transfer, and vehicle kinematics through hands-on building.

← All projects

Overview

Exposing children to STEM concepts at an early age is vital to fostering interest and inspiring future engineering pathways. To make STEM engaging, our team conceptualized an educational Go Kart kit that allows young users to assemble key components—including the wheels, steering wheel, and seat—to learn how mechanical parts interface to form a functional vehicle.

The concept features an exposed motor encased in a clear, heat-safe protective shield, enabling kids to safely observe internal mechanical motion. The vehicle converts user-generated pedal power into mechanical motor energy to drive the cart forward, teaching fundamental principles of energy transfer. Additionally, the kit includes customizable paint and decals to encourage creative personalization.

My Contribution

  • Steering Subsystem Design: Spearheaded the complete design of the steering system, ensuring functional kinematic linkages and smooth rotational response.
  • Subsystem Integration: Designed all component interfaces and mounting geometric tolerances to seamlessly integrate the steering assembly with the chassis and motor subsystems developed by team members.
  • CAD Modeling & Kinematic Verification: Modeled all custom linkage components, mounting brackets, and steering column geometries in CAD to verify kinematic clearance and full functional assembly alignment.
